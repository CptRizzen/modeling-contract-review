Risk Analysis

🔴 Critical — Physical Appearance Control (Section 8.3)

"Agency may require Model to modify appearance including but not limited to hair color, hair length, weight, and grooming at Agency's reasonable discretion to meet client requirements."
  • Issue: The agency can require you to change your hair and your weight. These are changes to your body, and this clause asks for no consent.
  • Market Standard: Written consent for each change; you keep final say over your body.
  • Redline: Add — "All appearance modifications require Model's prior written consent. Model's refusal to consent shall not constitute grounds for termination."
  • Negotiability: High — this is a reasonable ask and most agencies will accept it.

🔴 Critical — "In Perpetuity" Image Rights (Section 6.2)

"...irrevocable, worldwide, royalty-free license to use Model's image, likeness, name, and voice in perpetuity across all media now known or hereafter developed, including... the training... of machine learning and generative systems."
  • Issue: Your face, forever, anywhere, for one shoot fee — explicitly including AI training on your likeness.
  • Redline: Replace "in perpetuity" with "for two (2) years from the applicable shoot" and add "excluding use in AI training datasets or generative AI applications."
