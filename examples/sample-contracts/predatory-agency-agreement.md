## EXCLUSIVE MODEL REPRESENTATION AGREEMENT

This Exclusive Model Representation Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of
____________, 20____ (the "Effective Date") by and between **Northvane Model Management,
LLC**, a New York limited liability company ("Agency"), and ____________________ ("Model").

### 1. ENGAGEMENT

1.1 Model hereby engages Agency as Model's sole and exclusive representative for all
modeling, promotional, commercial, print, digital, runway, influencer, and related
services of any kind.

1.2 Model acknowledges that Agency makes no guarantee, representation, or warranty of any
kind regarding the number, frequency, or value of engagements Agency may obtain, and Model
waives any claim arising from Agency's failure to obtain any engagement.

### 2. TERM

2.1 The initial term of this Agreement shall be three (3) years from the Effective Date.

2.2 This Agreement shall automatically renew for successive one (1) year periods unless
either party provides written notice of non-renewal not less than thirty (30) days prior to
the expiration of the then-current term.

### 3. COMMISSION

3.1 Agency shall be entitled to a commission of twenty-two percent (22%) of the gross
booking fee for each engagement, plus applicable service charges as set forth in Schedule A.

3.2 Agency shall additionally be entitled to its full commission on any engagement booked
by Model directly, whether or not Agency participated in procuring such engagement.

3.3 Agency shall be entitled to its full commission on any engagement performed within
twelve (12) months following termination of this Agreement with any client with whom Model
was introduced, submitted, or otherwise made known during the term.

### 4. EXCLUSIVITY

4.1 During the term, Model shall not render services of any kind described in Section 1.1
for any person or entity other than through Agency, worldwide, in all categories and all
media, without Agency's prior written consent, which may be withheld in Agency's sole
discretion.

4.2 Model shall refer all inquiries received directly by Model to Agency within
twenty-four (24) hours.

### 5. FEES AND EXPENSES

5.1 Model agrees to pay Agency a composite card production fee of Three Hundred Fifty
Dollars ($350.00) upon execution of this Agreement, which fee is non-refundable.

5.2 Agency may deduct from Model's earnings all costs incurred by Agency in connection with
Model's career, including without limitation test shoots, digitals, portfolio development,
messenger and shipping charges, travel, accommodations, website hosting, and administrative
processing, without prior notice to or approval from Model.

### 6. IMAGE AND LIKENESS

6.1 Model grants Agency the right to use Model's name, image, likeness, and biographical
information in Agency's promotional materials.

6.2 Model grants Agency and its clients a non-exclusive, irrevocable, worldwide, royalty-free
license to use Model's image, likeness, name, and voice in perpetuity across all media now
known or hereafter developed, including without limitation for the training, development, and
operation of machine learning and generative systems.

6.3 All photographs, digitals, and test images produced during the term, whether or not paid
for by Model, shall remain the sole property of Agency.

### 7. PAYMENT

7.1 Agency shall remit payment to Model within a reasonable time following Agency's receipt
of payment from the applicable client, less commissions, service charges, and deductions.

7.2 Agency shall have no obligation to provide an itemized accounting of deductions except
upon written request, and not more than once per calendar year.

### 8. MODEL OBLIGATIONS

8.1 Model shall maintain Model's appearance, measurements, and general physical condition in
a manner satisfactory to Agency throughout the term.

8.2 Model shall be available for all engagements procured by Agency and shall not
unreasonably decline any engagement.

8.3 Agency may require Model to modify Model's appearance, including but not limited to hair
color, hair length, weight, and grooming, at Agency's reasonable discretion to meet client
requirements.

### 9. REPRESENTATIONS

9.1 Model represents that Model is over the age of eighteen (18) or, if under eighteen, that
a parent or legal guardian has executed this Agreement on Model's behalf.

9.2 Model represents that Model is not party to any other representation agreement.

### 10. CONFIDENTIALITY AND NON-DISPARAGEMENT

10.1 Each party shall maintain the confidentiality of the other party's non-public business
information.

10.2 Neither party shall make any disparaging statement regarding the other party during the
term or at any time thereafter.

### 11. TERMINATION

11.1 Agency may terminate this Agreement at any time, for any reason or no reason, upon
fifteen (15) days written notice to Model.

11.2 In the event of early termination by Model, Model shall forfeit commissions equivalent
to six (6) months of average monthly bookings based on the prior twelve (12) months.

### 12. INDEMNIFICATION

12.1 Each party shall indemnify the other against third-party claims arising from such
party's breach of this Agreement, provided that Model's indemnification obligations shall not
be subject to any cap or limitation.

### 13. DISPUTE RESOLUTION

13.1 Any dispute arising under this Agreement shall be resolved by binding arbitration
administered in New York County, New York. Model waives any right to participate in a class,
collective, or representative action.

### 14. GENERAL

14.1 This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of New York.

14.2 Agency may amend the terms of Schedule A at any time upon written notice to Model.

14.3 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes all
prior understandings.
